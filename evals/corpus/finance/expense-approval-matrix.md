# Expense approval matrix

## Thresholds

Expenses up to 500 EUR are approved by the direct manager. Expenses between 500 and 5,000 EUR require the department head. Anything above 5,000 EUR requires the CFO, and above 50,000 EUR requires board approval.

## Vendors

New vendors must pass a KYC check before the first payment. Vendor payments run on the 15th and the last business day of each month.

## Salary bands

Engineering salary bands for 2026: level 1 is 42,000 to 55,000 EUR, level 2 is 55,000 to 72,000 EUR, level 3 is 72,000 to 95,000 EUR. Bands are reviewed every January.
