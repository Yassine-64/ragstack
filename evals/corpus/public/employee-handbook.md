# Employee handbook

## Working hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Core hours during which everyone must be reachable are 10:00 to 16:00. Flexible start times between 8:00 and 10:00 are allowed with manager approval.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Remote days must be recorded in the HR portal before the start of the week. Fully remote arrangements require a written agreement signed by the department head.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the first quarter of the following year. Leave requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

## Expenses

Travel expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of submission. Meals are capped at 45 EUR per day during travel. Receipts are mandatory for any expense above 10 EUR.
