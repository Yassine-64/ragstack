# IT security policy

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and rotated every 180 days. Password managers are mandatory; the company provides a 1Password business account to every employee.

## Multi-factor authentication

MFA is required on every system that supports it. Hardware keys (YubiKey 5) are issued to engineers and finance staff; other staff use the Okta Verify app.

## Laptops

Laptops are encrypted with FileVault or BitLocker and must be locked when unattended. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@company.example within 2 hours.

## Incident reporting

Suspected phishing or security incidents are reported through the #security channel or the security@ mailbox. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues.
