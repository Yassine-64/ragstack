# Performance review process

## Cycle

Reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Self-assessments are due two weeks before the review meeting. Managers calibrate ratings with their peers before sharing them.

## Ratings

Ratings use a four-point scale: needs improvement, meets expectations, exceeds expectations, outstanding. An outstanding rating two cycles in a row triggers a promotion review.

## Disciplinary procedure

A formal warning is valid for 12 months. Two formal warnings within that period may lead to termination after a hearing with HR and the employee's manager.
